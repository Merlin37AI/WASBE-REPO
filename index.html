Services

Three areas of consulting

Use one on its own, or combine them when you are expanding into a new market.

International brokerage

Find the right counterparty abroad and close the deal.

  • Global supplier sourcingA shortlist of vetted suppliers and manufacturers, with quotes compared side by side.
  • Buyer and distributor introductionsMeetings with importers, distributors and buyers in the markets you want to enter.
  • Deal negotiationWe handle pricing, terms and samples through to a signed contract.
  • Deal follow-throughWe coordinate paperwork and hand over to your logistics and finance providers.

International marketing

Reach customers in new countries and know what worked.

  • Market research and positioningCustomers, competitors and pricing in your target country, and how to stand out.
  • Go-to-market planChannels, messaging and a launch calendar for a new market.
  • Lead generation campaignsTargeted email and LinkedIn outreach, with monthly reports on meetings booked.
  • Multilingual campaigns and eventsMessaging adapted for each audience, plus planning for trade shows abroad.

Business management

Run the business well while it grows.

  • Operations reviewWe map your processes, find the bottlenecks and hand you a 30-day action plan.
  • Expansion planTarget market, route to market, team and a first-year budget.
  • Business plan and financial modelA clear plan for investors, lenders or your own board.
  • Cost and performance reviewWe compare supplier costs with the market and set up simple monthly reporting.
